Getting good results

Film from the side, with the camera at about hip height and the skier's whole body in the frame. The angles are measured in the image plane, so they are only meaningful when the skier moves across the picture, not toward or away from the camera. One skier per video works best.

Each joint angle is the angle between the two body segments at that joint, so 180° means fully straight. Trunk lean is measured from vertical, so 0° means upright. Ski poles are not tracked.

The arm and leg on the far side of the body are often hidden. The tool marks those frames as low confidence, and you should treat those values with care.
